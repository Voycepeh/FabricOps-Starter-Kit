# Governance & AI

This section explains how FabricOps Starter Kit keeps data product operations reliable, auditable, and AI-assisted.

## What you can do here

- Manage metadata and data contract expectations.
- Apply quality checks and rule workflows.
- Execute governance enrichment and reviewer handoff.
- Track drift signals and contract-aligned change behavior.

1. Confirm **Metadata & Contracts** baselines.
2. Configure and review **Data Quality** rules.
3. Run the **Governance Workflow** enrichment path.
